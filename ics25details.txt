🚀 Event Overview
The Insturix Creators Summit 2025 (ICS'25) is India's largest student-led creator-tech
summit, organized by Insturix, a Delhi-based AI platform revolutionizing content creation.
Founded by Nimit Jain, Insturix empowers creators with flagship tools like Editron
(AI-powered video editing for seamless Reels and Shorts), Alyzitron (advanced content
analytics), Musitron (AI-driven music creation for videos), and Thinkforge (creative ideation
and scripting assistant). Note: Shield, a secondary tool for content protection, is also
available but not a flagship focus.
This two-day extravaganza unites 800+ attendees—including aspiring and established
creators, edutainment pros, gamers, brands, entrepreneurs, and students—to explore AI's
transformative role in the creator economy. From live competitions to networking hubs,
ICS'25 is the ultimate launchpad for collaborations, skill-building, and future-proofing your
content game.
Why ICS'25?
● Scale: Over 800 live attendees + 50,000+ digital reach via streams and social media
(@insturix).
● Impact: Past events sparked 200+ creator-brand collabs and viral content.
● Inclusivity: Student-led, accessible pricing, and hybrid elements for broader
participation.
Organized by: Insturix (insturix.com)
Registration: Secure your spot at https://insturix.com/ics25 – spots filling fast!

22-23).

● Watch/Play: Live stream on Instagram/YouTube; onsite in Gaming/Esports Zone with
pro setups (PCs for Valorant, mobile for BGMI).
● Rules & Tech: Mobile-only for BGMI (no emulators); standard Valorant competitive
maps. Full FAQ on site for anti-cheat, disconnects, and conduct.
Why Join? Connect with gaming creators like BeastBoyShub, compete for prizes, and create
viral content with AI tools like Editron and Musitron.
