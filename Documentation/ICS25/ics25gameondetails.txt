GameOn Esports Tournament FAQ - Insturix Creators Summit 2025
🕹️ General Event Information
What is the Esports Sub-Event at Creator’s Summit 2025?
The Esports Sub-Event is GameOn, a high-stakes gaming tournament integrated into the Insturix Creators Summit 2025 (ICS'25), focusing on competitive play in popular titles while connecting creators and gamers.
When and where will the tournament take place?
The tournament qualifiers are online: November 8, 2025. The finals will take place on November 15, 2025 (live and/or streamed). The original ICS'25 on-site summit remains scheduled for November 22 at IIIT Delhi.
What games will be featured in this esports event?
The featured games are Valorant (5v5) and BGMI (4v4).
What is the DTV (Date, Time, Venue) of the event?
Date: Finals on November 22, 2025 (full ICS'25 schedule).
Time: Tournament sessions integrated into the summit's agenda (specific times TBD; expect peak hours during the Gaming/Esports Zone).
Venue: Indraprastha Institute of Information Technology Delhi (IIIT Delhi), Okhla Industrial Estate Phase III, New Delhi.
Who can participate in the esports tournament?
Participants must be registered attendees or creators affiliated with ICS'25, aged 18+ (or with guardian consent for minors), and form teams for the respective games.
Can fans or attendees watch the matches live?
Yes, fans and attendees can watch the finals live in the dedicated Gaming/Esports Zone at IIIT Delhi during ICS'25.
Will the event be streamed online or recorded?
Yes, the finals will be streamed live on Insturix's social media channels (Instagram, YouTube), with recordings available post-event.
Is there an entry fee to participate in the tournament?
Yes, the entry fee is ₹500 per team.
What is included in the entry fee?
The entry fee covers access to qualifiers, finals participation, and entry to ICS'25 (one complimentary Bronze ticket per team member).
Can individual players join, or do I need a full team?
Full teams are required: 5 players for Valorant and 4 players for BGMI. Individuals can join open team formation via the registration portal.
Is there a registration limit for the number of teams?
Yes, limited to 120 teams total (90 teams already registered, 30 slots left).
When is the last day to register?
Registration closes on October 31, 2025, or when slots fill up, whichever comes first.
How can I register for the event?
Register via the official ICS'25 website: https://insturix.com/ics25 (select GameOn Tournament section).
What payment methods are accepted for registration?
Online payments via UPI, credit/debit cards, net banking (processed through Razorpay or similar gateway on the site).
What happens after I register?
You'll receive a confirmation email with team ID, qualifier details, and a dashboard link to manage your entry and complete tasks.
Can I update or edit my team details after registration?
Yes, edits (e.g., roster changes) are allowed until October 25, 2025, via the registration dashboard.
What is the policy on registration refunds or cancellations?
Refunds are available up to October 25, 2025, minus a 10% processing fee. No refunds after qualifiers begin.
How can I track my team’s status after registration?
Use the team dashboard on https://insturix.com/ics25 or follow updates on Insturix's social media (@insturix).
💰 Rewards & Cashback System
How does the ₹500 entry fee and cashback system work?
Pay the full ₹500 entry fee upfront. Complete 3 Creator Tasks to earn ₹350 cashback, reducing your net fee to ₹150.
What are the “Creator Tasks” I need to complete for cashback?
Tasks include: (1) Create and share a Reel promoting ICS'25 using #GameOnICS25 #ics25 #insturix, (2) Get 3 friends registered and paid, (3) Post a LinkedIn post about insturix and ICS25 + ICS25 GameOn

 How much cashback can I earn by completing the tasks?
₹350 cashback per person upon completing all 3 tasks.
How will the cashback be credited?
Via UPI or bank transfer to the team captain's registered account within 3 days of task verification.
Are there any deadlines for completing the tasks?
Tasks must be completed and submitted by October 30, 2025, before qualifiers.
🎮 Valorant Tournament Specifics
What is the format of the Valorant tournament?
Single-elimination bracket for qualifiers (best-of-1), double-elimination for finals (best-of-3).
How many players are allowed per Valorant team?
5 players per team.
Will substitute players be allowed?
Yes. Each team may list 1 substitute. Substitutes should be added at the team's discretion and registered in advance. Substitutes follow the same payment process; they may request a refund before qualifiers begin. No refunds after qualifiers begin.
What is the total prize pool for Valorant?
Part of the overall ₹25,000 prize pool (specific split: 1st: ₹7,000, 2nd: ₹4,000, 3rd: ₹2,000; remainder for lower placements).
Will there be specific agent or map restrictions?
Standard competitive rules; no custom agent bans beyond official patches.
What are the official map pools for Valorant?
Current competitive map pool: Ascent, Bind, Haven, Icebox, Lotus, Sunset (as per latest Riot updates).
Are custom skins or overlays allowed?
Yes, custom skins permitted; overlays allowed if they don't interfere with game integrity.
What is the rule on communication during matches?
Voice comms allowed within team only; no external coaching or sharing info.
How will match seeding be determined?
Based on qualifier performance and random draw for initial brackets.
How will ties or overtime be handled?
Standard Valorant overtime rules: 6-round OT, alternating pistol eco.
What happens if a player disconnects mid-match?
Pause up to 5 minutes for reconnect; technical loss after 2 disconnects per match.
How will disputes or rule violations be addressed?
Reported to referees via in-game ticket system; reviewed by admin panel with video evidence.
🔫 BGMI Tournament Specifics
What is the format of the BGMI tournament?
Squad-based classic mode, round-robin qualifiers, knockout finals.
How many players are allowed per BGMI team?
4 players per team.
Will substitute players be allowed?
Yes. Each team may list 1 substitute. Substitutes should be added at the team's discretion and registered in advance. Substitutes follow the same payment process; they may request a refund before qualifiers begin. No refunds after qualifiers begin.
What is the total prize pool for BGMI?
Part of the overall ₹25,000 prize pool (specific split: 1st: ₹7,000, 2nd: ₹4,000, 3rd: ₹2,000; remainder for lower placements).
Are emulators allowed for BGMI?
No, only mobile devices; no emulators to ensure fair play.
What are the official map pools or match types?
Erangel, Miramar, Sanhok, Vikendi; classic squad matches.
Will there be TPP or FPP modes?
TPP (Third-Person Perspective) mode only.
How will match rooms and codes be shared?
Via official Discord server and email to team captains 30 minutes before each match.
What happens if a player gets disconnected?
5-minute grace period for reconnect; team plays short-handed otherwise.
What anti-cheat measures will be in place?
BGMI's built-in anti-cheat, plus admin monitoring via spectator mode and reports.
How will the points system or ranking be based?
Points: 10 for 1st, 8 for 2nd, down to 1 for 10th; survival and kills bonus.
