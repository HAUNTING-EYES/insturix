## McDonald's — "Morning Ritual" (30s Brand Ad)

**Production Notes:**
- Target: Instagram Reels / TikTok
- Color Grade: Warm golden tones, sunrise palette
- Pacing: Building energy, slow start to upbeat finish
- Music: Start with soft acoustic guitar, build to upbeat pop

---

**Scene 1: The Alarm (0-4 seconds)**

Visuals: Close-up of a phone alarm going off at 6:30 AM on a nightstand. Soft morning light filtering through curtains.
Audio: VO: "Every morning starts the same way..."
Camera: Slow push-in on the phone screen
Music Direction: Soft fingerpicked acoustic guitar, gentle and warm
SFX: Phone alarm chime, sheets rustling

**Transition: Soft Cut**

**Scene 2: The Drive (4-8 seconds)**

Visuals: A red sedan driving through quiet suburban streets at dawn. Golden hour light streaking across the windshield.
Audio: VO: "The same route. The same anticipation."
Camera: Tracking shot following the car from the side
Music Direction: Guitar continues, light percussion enters
SFX: Car engine hum, birds chirping

**Transition: Dissolve**

**Scene 3: The Golden Arches (8-12 seconds)**

Visuals: The iconic McDonald's golden arches sign glowing against a pink-orange sunrise sky. Shot from the car approaching the drive-through.
Audio: VO: "Until that first glimpse of gold."
Camera: Low angle looking up at the arches
Music Direction: Music lifts — add strings, tempo increases slightly
On-Screen Text: "The Golden Standard"

**Transition: Hard Cut**

**Scene 4: The Order (12-16 seconds)**

Visuals: Extreme close-up of a hand reaching through the drive-through window, receiving a warm McDonald's bag. Steam rising from the bag. Worker smiling in the background.
Audio: VO: "Fresh. Warm. Exactly what you needed."
Camera: Tight macro shot, shallow depth of field
Music Direction: Full band kicks in — drums, bass, bright pop feel
SFX: Paper bag crinkling, cash register beep

**Transition: Zoom Punch**

**Scene 5: The Unwrap — Montage (16-22 seconds)**

Quick cuts:
- Shot 1: Hands unwrapping an Egg McMuffin on the car dashboard, cheese stretching
- Shot 2: Close-up of crispy hash brown being bitten, crunch visible
- Shot 3: Hot coffee cup with steam swirling, McDonald's logo visible
- Shot 4: Person taking first bite, eyes closing in satisfaction

Audio: VO: "The McMuffin. The hash brown. The coffee. The moment."
Music Direction: Upbeat pop at full energy, syncopated rhythm
SFX: Wrapper crinkling, crispy crunch, coffee pour

**Transition: Flash**

**Scene 6: The Smile (22-26 seconds)**

Visuals: Medium shot of the driver sitting in the parked car, morning sunlight on their face, genuine smile while chewing. Dashboard breakfast spread visible.
Audio: VO: "This isn't just breakfast."
Camera: Steady medium shot, golden hour backlight
Music Direction: Music sustains at peak energy

**Transition: Dip to Black**

**Scene 7: Logo Reveal (26-28 seconds)**

Visuals: McDonald's golden arches logo on a clean warm-toned background with the tagline.
Audio: VO: "I'm lovin' it."
Camera: Static centered frame
Music Direction: Iconic "ba da ba ba baa" jingle
On-Screen Text: "i'm lovin' it"
SFX: Signature McDonald's jingle notes

**Scene 8: End Card (28-30 seconds)**

Visuals: Text card — app download CTA on McDonald's red background.
Camera: Static
On-Screen Text: "Order ahead on the McDonald's app"
On-Screen Text: "#MorningRitual"
Music Direction: Jingle fades out
